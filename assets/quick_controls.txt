 QUICK CONTROLS
    2-Finger Long Press = app menu
     ? = help
    ?? = default keymap ("<>" to scroll)

 Game screen 9-grid touch overlay:
     ↖ | ↑ | ↗
    ---+---+---  Tap to move / attack
     ← | ↻ | →   Center tap waits a turn
    ---+---+---  Drag to scroll text
     ↙ | ↓ | ↘

    Tab / o = auto-fight / auto-explore
    ./5 = wait a turn / long rest
      i = inventory
      m = skills training
    w/W = wield / Wear armor
    g/d = get / drop
    r/q = read scroll / quaff potion
    f/Q = fire / Quiver
    z/I/M = cast / list / Memorize spells

 Check help (?) for full controls list.

 * NEW PLAYERS *
    If it's your first time playing DCSS,
    know that it's the hardest yet fairest RPG
    ever made. There's a BIG learning curve!

    Start with a tough, easy combo like a
    Minotaur or Troll Fighter.

    Explore cautiously, examine everything,
    and don't be afraid to run from danger.

    Use your items to survive, don't hoard!

    Focus on training useful skills, and
    try to survive until you reach the Temple
    with powerful Gods to worship. GOOD LUCK!
