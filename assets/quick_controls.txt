 QUICK CONTROLS
     ? = help
    ?? = keyboard controls

 Touch screen edges for overlay controls:
     ↖ | ↑ | ↗   • Drag to scroll text
    ---+---+---  • Tap or hold to move / attack
     ← | ↻ | →   • Tap center to wait a turn
    ---+---+---  • Hold center to rest
     ↙ | ↓ | ↘   • Pinch to zoom map

 Important Keys:
        Tab = auto-fight
          o = auto-explore
        ./5 = wait a turn / long rest
          i = inventory
          m = skills training
        w/W = wield / Wear armor
        g/d = get / drop
        r/q = read scroll / quaff potion
        f/Q = fire / Quiver
      z/I/M = cast / list / Memorize spells

 Use 2-finger long press to access App Menu

 Press help (?) twice for extended controls list.

 * NEW PLAYERS *
    If it's your first time playing DCSS,
    know that it's the hardest yet fairest RPG
    ever made. There's a BIG learning curve!

    Start with a strong, easy combo like a
    Minotaur or Troll Fighter.

    Train skills, explore, examine everything.
    Use your items to survive, don't hoard!
    And ALWAYS be ready to run from danger.

    Train your skills wisely, and find a
    powerful God to worship. GOOD LUCK!
