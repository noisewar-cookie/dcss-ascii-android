Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup ASCII (DCSS) is a free, open-source roguelike about exploring an endlessly unique dungeon, fighting monsters, and escaping with the Orb of Zot. This pure-ASCII Android port provides a classic, streamlined experience.

DCSS is considered by true roguelike fans to be one of the most tactical, difficult, yet fair dungeon crawl RPGs ever made.

Happy Crawling!


GAMEPLAY
• 27 playable species and 24 backgrounds — endless ways to die!
• Hand-crafted dungeon branches: Orcish Mines, Slime Pits, Hell, the Abyss, and more.
• Hardcore turn-based, single-player tactics.
• Permadeath: every choice matters.
• Hundreds of monsters, spells, items, and gods to worship.

CONTROLS
• Customizable on-screen virtual keyboard tuned for DCSS commands.
• 9-grid control overlay for easy character control.
• Tap to move, swipe to scroll.

PRIVACY & PERMISSIONS
• Offline only.
• No ads, tracking, data collection, or account required.

ABOUT THIS PORT
Wraps DCSS 0.34.1 (upstream: github.com/crawl/crawl). Forked from Michael Barlow's original Android port and updated for modern Android. Licensed under GPL-2.0-or-later. Game content under its respective licenses (see in-app credits).
