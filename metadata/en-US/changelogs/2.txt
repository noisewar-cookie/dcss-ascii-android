First release of DCSS ASCII, a fork of Michael Barlow's Android port.

• Updated to DCSS 0.34.1 (latest).
• Two-finger long press for app menu.
• Improved 9-grid overlay controls.
• Revamped UI for portrait view.
• Manage saves and config files.
• Faster asset loading.
• Quick Controls guide for new players.

Bug Fixes:
• Supports Android 15+, some compatibility for 13, 14, etc.
• Auto-save now more reliable between app resume states.
• Fixed missing font glyphs.
